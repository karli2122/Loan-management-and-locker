How PayLock Pro Works

From client onboarding to automated collections - a streamlined workflow designed for IT professionals.

1

Create Your Admin Account

Sign up in under 2 minutes. Configure your business profile, set your preferred currency, and customize lock screen messages. Your dashboard is immediately ready with full analytics.

2

Add Clients & Create Loan Plans

Register clients individually or bulk-import via CSV. Define loan plans with custom interest rates (day-count based), payment frequencies, and currency. Generate professional PDF contracts automatically.

3

Provision Client Devices

Enroll client devices using QR codes or NFC tags. The PayLock Client app installs with Device Admin or Device Owner privileges, enabling remote device management and lock screen control.

4

Automated Payment Scheduling

Set up recurring payment schedules with configurable reminders. The system automatically sends push notifications, emails, and Telegram messages before each due date. Late fees are calculated and applied automatically.

5

Intelligent Device Security

If a payment is overdue past the grace period, the device is automatically locked with a custom message. Temporary locks, granular lock reasons, and a full audit trail give you complete control. Unlock instantly when payment is received.

6

Monitor & Analyze

Track everything from your real-time dashboard: collection rates, overdue accounts, client credit scores, GPS locations, and device heartbeats. Export reports as PDF or CSV. Schedule automated email reports for your team.
