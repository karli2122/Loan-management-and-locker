Enterprise-Grade Features

Everything you need to manage loans, devices, and team operations securely

Remote Device Lock/Unlock

Instantly lock or unlock client devices remotely. Support for both Device Admin and Device Owner modes with full kiosk capabilities.

Automated Payment Scheduling

Set up recurring payment reminders with daily, weekly, or monthly schedules. Auto-processing sends notifications when payments are due.

Analytics & Reports

Revenue trends, profit analysis, collection rates, and loan distribution charts. Export reports to PDF or CSV. Schedule automatic email reports.

Team Management

Multi-admin support with role-based permissions. Activity logging tracks every action across your enterprise.

16 Languages (i18n)

Full portal translation for Estonian, English, Norwegian, Swedish, Danish, Finnish, Latvian, Lithuanian, German, Czech, Polish, Spanish, French, Italian, and more.

Telegram Notifications

One-way Telegram bot integration for instant payment reminders, overdue alerts, and administrative notifications.

Bank Statement OCR

Upload and parse bank statements automatically. Supported formats: PDF, ASICE. Powered by Tesseract OCR engine.

NFC/QR Provisioning

Generate QR codes and NFC payloads for quick device setup. Download QR images or copy NFC data directly to tags.

Bulk CSV Import

Import hundreds of clients at once from CSV files. Validate data before import with detailed error reporting.

Google Drive Backup

Automatic cloud backups to Google Drive. Keep your loan data, client records, and payment history safe and accessible.

Stripe Payments

Accept online payments through Stripe integration. Process subscription fees and loan repayments securely.

Document Storage

Upload and manage client documents. Attach ID copies, contracts, and proof of income directly to client profiles.

About PayLock Pro

Innovative IT solutions from Estonia, built for secure loan management

Pioneering Secure Loan Technology from Estonia

PayLock Pro is an innovative fintech solution developed in Estonia, one of Europe's leading IT hubs. Our platform combines cutting-edge device management technology with comprehensive loan administration tools, providing a secure and efficient way for lending businesses to operate.

Founded by a team of experienced IT professionals, we understand the unique challenges that modern loan providers face. Our Estonian heritage means we build with privacy-first design, GDPR compliance, and the digital-forward thinking that has made Estonia a global leader in e-governance and secure digital services.

Our mission is to provide innovative, enterprise-grade tools that make loan management simple, transparent, and secure for businesses of every size. From small micro-lenders to large financial institutions, PayLock Pro scales with your needs while maintaining the highest standards of IT security and data protection.
